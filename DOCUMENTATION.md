## 1. What PrepVista AI Is

**PrepVista AI is an AI-powered mock-interview and placement-readiness platform.** It runs realistic, personalized interviews with candidates, grades every answer against a professional rubric, and turns the results into clear, decision-ready numbers.

Two numbers sit at the heart of the product:

- **Placement Readiness Score (0–100)** — a single answer to "is this candidate ready to be placed right now?"
- **Hiring Probability** — the readiness score expressed as intuitive, per-company estimates (for example, "TCS 82%, Product company 61%") that a candidate and a placement officer both immediately understand.

Rather than handing users generic study material, PrepVista personalizes the entire session to the individual: it reads their background, asks questions that fit their field and experience, listens to their actual answers, and adapts in real time — just like a human interviewer would.

**What makes the product distinctive:**
- It **listens and adapts** — the next question is shaped by what the candidate just said, not a fixed script.
- It **grades fairly and consistently** — every answer is scored against a structured rubric, not a vague impression.
- It **explains itself** — scores are backed by specific evidence from the candidate's own answers.
- It **tells you what to do next** — every session ends with concrete, personalized coaching.
- It **works at scale** — a college can run hundreds of unique interviews simultaneously during a placement drive.

## 3. The Core Experience: An AI Mock Interview

A PrepVista interview feels like a real conversation with a professional interviewer.

### 3.1 Getting started
The candidate uploads a resume and selects a difficulty level. The platform instantly understands their background and prepares a tailored interview — with the first question ready the moment they click "Start", so there is no waiting.

### 3.2 The conversation
The interview proceeds turn by turn. The candidate answers by **voice or by typing**. The AI responds naturally, asks follow-up questions grounded in the candidate's specific answers, and moves through a planned set of topics that matter for their field.

### 3.3 Difficulty levels
Candidates choose how challenging the interview should feel:
- **Auto** — adapts to the resume and to answers as the interview unfolds.
- **Basic** — simpler, more direct questions with gentle follow-ups; ideal for building confidence.
- **Medium** — balanced depth without overly easy warm-ups.
- **Difficult** — sharper, tougher questioning for strong candidates who want real pressure.

### 3.4 A supportive, human pace
If a candidate goes quiet or struggles, the AI simplifies the question rather than penalizing them — the goal is to measure knowledge, not to trip people up. The tone shifts with the plan and difficulty, from an encouraging coach to a demanding hiring panel.

### 3.5 The finish
When the interview ends, the platform produces a complete evaluation: an overall score, a placement-readiness rating, a topic-by-topic breakdown, and personalized coaching — with a downloadable report for paid plans.

## 4. The AI Interviewer

The interviewer is the "living" part of the product. It is designed to feel like a real person who is genuinely paying attention.

### 4.1 Personalized questions
Every question is built around the candidate's actual background and the topic being tested. Two different candidates never get the same interview.

### 4.2 It listens and adapts
The AI reads each answer, picks out the concrete facts the candidate mentioned, and anchors its next question to those facts. This makes the interview feel like a real dialogue rather than a checklist being read aloud.

### 4.3 Planned topic coverage
Interviews aren't random. Each session is planned to cover a balanced mix of areas — self-introduction, technical depth, project ownership, communication, problem-solving, behavioral judgment, situational judgment, creative thinking, and comfort working with AI tools. Longer plans cover more ground.

### 4.4 Field-specific technical questions
Technical questions are matched to the candidate's engineering branch. A Computer Science student is tested on the fundamentals that matter for their field; a Civil student is tested on theirs. The result is a technical score that is meaningful within a branch and fairly comparable across candidates.

### 4.5 It never repeats itself
The interviewer recognizes when a topic has already been covered and avoids asking the same thing twice — even when the wording would be different. It keeps the conversation moving forward.

### 4.6 It remembers across sessions
When a candidate returns for another interview, the platform remembers which topics they've already faced and prioritizes new ground — so a second interview genuinely feels different from the first, and coverage builds up over time.

### 4.7 Built for placement-drive scale
The interviewer is engineered so that hundreds of students can start unique interviews at the same moment — during a live campus placement drive — without slowdowns and without everyone receiving similar questions.

## 5. The AI Evaluator: How Answers Are Graded

The evaluator turns a natural conversation into fair, consistent, defensible scores. It behaves like a strict but reasonable examiner — predictable, evidence-based, and hard to game.

### 5.1 Two dimensions per answer
Every answer is scored on both **what** was said (technical depth and correctness) and **how** it was said (communication and clarity) — separately — so a strong idea delivered poorly, or a weak idea delivered smoothly, are each judged accurately.

### 5.2 Evidence-backed scoring
Scores are justified with **specific evidence pulled from the candidate's own words**. The platform aggressively filters out vague or generic praise, so feedback points to something real the candidate actually said.

### 5.3 Consistent, trustworthy numbers
Final scores are calculated deterministically, so the same performance always produces the same result. Numbers don't drift or contradict the dashboard.

### 5.4 Fair across skill levels
Grading strictness adapts to the candidate's level so beginners aren't unfairly punished by advanced standards, while stronger candidates get genuinely rigorous evaluation.

### 5.5 A full professional evaluation
Beyond a single score, each session produces a complete, HR-grade assessment covering strengths, gaps, ideal-answer guidance, integrity and ownership signals, and a topic-level breakdown — the kind of write-up a candidate could take to a mentor or recruiter.

## 6. Placement Readiness & Hiring Probability

This is the capability colleges specifically buy the platform for: turning interview performance into placement decisions.

### 6.1 The Placement Readiness Score
Every candidate and session gets a single **0–100 readiness score** that answers, plainly, "how ready is this person to be placed?" Under the hood it rolls up all the fine-grained scoring into a handful of **placement-weighted competency areas**, giving more weight to the things that actually determine hiring outcomes.

### 6.2 Readiness tiers
The score maps to four intuitive tiers used consistently for individuals and cohorts:
- **Ready**
- **Almost Ready**
- **Developing**
- **At Risk**

The same logic is used whether you're looking at one student or an entire batch, so the numbers are directly comparable.

### 6.3 Hiring probability per company type
The readiness score is translated into **per-company hiring probabilities** — for example, showing a higher chance at a service company and a lower chance at a top product company. This makes an abstract number instantly actionable: a candidate knows exactly where they stand and what to aim for.

### 6.4 It learns from real outcomes
Placement teams can feed **real hiring results** back into the platform — who actually got placed, and where. The platform uses those outcomes to **calibrate its probability estimates**, so predictions get more accurate for each company over time. Until enough real data exists for a company, the platform uses a sensible default estimate.

## 7. Voice Interviews

PrepVista lets candidates speak their answers, just like a real interview.

### 7.1 Works on every browser
Voice interviews are transcribed reliably regardless of the candidate's browser or device — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge — so no one is blocked by hardware or software quirks.

### 7.2 Hardware-resilient understanding
Web microphones and speech recognition are imperfect. The platform actively repairs transcripts — fixing stretched-out sounds and, importantly, correcting mis-heard names of colleges, companies, tools, and projects by cross-referencing the candidate's own resume. Candidates are judged on their knowledge, not on their microphone.

### 7.3 A dispute and audit trail
Interview audio can be securely retained so a session can be reviewed later if a score is ever questioned. Retained audio is kept privately and accessible only through secure, time-limited links.

### 7.4 Type instead, if preferred
Candidates who prefer to type can answer in text at any time — the same intelligence applies either way.

## 8. Resume Intelligence

The resume is the starting point for personalization, and the platform understands it in seconds.

### 8.1 Accepts real-world resumes
Candidates can upload resumes as PDFs, Word documents, or even photos/scans — the platform reads image-based resumes as well as text ones.

### 8.2 Understands the candidate
It automatically identifies the candidate's field and experience, so the interviewer knows how to contextualize every question from the very first turn.

### 8.3 Branch-aware for students
For college students, the platform recognizes the department — even from messy, hand-typed entries and unusual abbreviations — and routes the student to the right technical question set. One odd entry never blocks onboarding a whole batch.

### 8.4 Safe by design
The platform screens uploaded resumes for hidden manipulation attempts, so no one can trick the AI into a favorable score by planting instructions in their document.

### 8.5 Always ready to start
If a resume is unusually complex, the platform falls back gracefully so the candidate can still begin their interview immediately.

## 13. Analytics for Colleges & Placement Officers

Colleges get decision-ready intelligence, not just raw data.

### 13.1 Cohort readiness at a glance
A traffic-light view shows, instantly, how many students are Ready, Almost Ready, Developing, or At Risk — for the whole college or any department, year, or batch.

## 14. Fairness, Integrity & Trust

For results to matter, they have to be fair and credible.

### 14.1 Consistent grading
Because scores are calculated deterministically against a fixed rubric, the same performance always earns the same result — no randomness, no drift.

### 14.2 Evidence over opinion
Every score is tied to concrete evidence from the candidate's answer, so feedback is defensible and specific rather than vague.

### 14.3 Judged on knowledge, not equipment
Transcript repair ensures candidates aren't penalized for a poor microphone or a mispronounced proper noun.

### 14.4 Interview integrity
During voice interviews, the platform monitors for integrity signals — such as a candidate leaving the frame or switching away from the interview — and records them, so results reflect genuine, unassisted performance.

### 14.5 A reviewable record
Retained audio and a full transcript mean any disputed score can be reviewed after the fact.

## 15. Privacy & Security Commitments

PrepVista handles candidate data — resumes, recordings, and performance — with care.

- **Personal data is protected.** Resumes, recordings, and answers are never exposed in error reports or logs, and are kept private.
- **Recordings are private by default.** Interview audio is stored securely and reachable only through secure, time-limited links — never publicly.
- **Manipulation is blocked.** Uploaded documents and answers are screened for hidden instructions designed to game the AI.
- **Abuse is contained.** The platform limits automated abuse and bot traffic so genuine users always get fast, reliable service.
- **Strong account safety.** Sign-in is protected against brute-force attempts and low-quality or throwaway sign-ups.
- **Enterprise-grade posture.** The platform is built to meet the security expectations of institutional and B2B buyers.
