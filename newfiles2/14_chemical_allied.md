### Chemical / Process Engineer — chemical_process · chem · core/product/general · fresher→
Designs and operates chemical processes.
**STACK** — Core (5): **mass & energy balances** (steady/unsteady, recycle/bypass/purge) · **thermodynamics** (phase equilibria, VLE, energy) · **fluid mechanics** (flow, pumps, pressure drop) · **heat & mass transfer** (HX, distillation, absorption) · **reaction engineering** (reactors — CSTR/PFR/batch, kinetics, conversion). Tools (3): Aspen Plus/HYSYS · MATLAB · P&ID. Emerging (2): process safety · sustainability/green chemistry · digital twins.
**ROUNDS** — technical → chemical depth → process scenario → HR.
**ASKABLE** — *Mass/energy balance:* `[E]` what is a mass balance. `[M]` balance with recycle/purge; degrees of freedom; energy balance on a unit. `[H]` unsteady-state balance; balance around a reactor + separator. *Thermo:* `[M]` VLE; Raoult's/Henry's law; bubble/dew point; heat of reaction. `[H]` phase diagrams; non-ideal behavior. *Transfer/unit ops:* `[E]` distillation basics. `[M]` heat exchanger (LMTD); distillation (McCabe-Thiele, reflux); absorption; drying. `[H]` design a column/HX; mass transfer coefficients. *Reaction engineering:* `[E]` CSTR vs PFR vs batch. `[M]` conversion; rate laws; reactor sizing; which reactor for a reaction. `[H]` multiple reactions/selectivity; non-isothermal reactors. *Fluid/safety:* `[M]` pump sizing; pressure drop; P&ID reading; relief valves. `[H]` process safety (HAZOP basics).
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: balances + unit ops + reactor design + safety. Weak: rote, can't set up a balance. **RED FLAGS** — can't do a mass balance; no safety awareness.
