# PrepVista Feature #02 — ROLE ROSTER (breadth across ALL departments)

> **How to read this.** Every role maps to a **family** (which supplies a default round structure +
> rubric + core skill list — Dossier Part 2) and the **departments** whose students are eligible. Roles
> are tiered:
> - **Tier A — Full-fidelity:** hand-verified weighted skill stack + round structure. Build first.
> - **Tier B — Template-mapped:** inherits its family template (usable day one), verified over time.
> - **Tier C — Generic family:** any role in `roles.json` (58 seeded) or requested runs its family
>   template — never a "role not covered" dead-end. A "Request full modeling" button records demand.
>
> **Families:** software_engineering · data_ai · infrastructure · quality_security · electronics_ece ·
> electrical_eee · mechanical · civil · chemical_process · business_product · design · finance_tech ·
> sales_engineering · writing_devrel · operations_support.
> **Dept keys:** cse · aids · aiml · ece · eee · mech · civil · cyber (+ it, chem, bio, mba/commerce, design as extensions).

## REVENUE & MOAT NOTE
- **Every department is covered** — including ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, and the business/
  design/finance roles that competitors ignore. Half of every campus is those students; serving them is
  both a moat and untapped demand.
- **Tier C guarantees no dead-ends** — any role runs its family template with a "generic" badge + a
  "Request full modeling" demand signal that prioritizes promotion.
- **Role × Company** (Dossier Part 6) multiplies this roster by the Feature #01 company roster into a
  combinatorial prep surface (~120 roles × ~150 companies) unique to owning both axes.
