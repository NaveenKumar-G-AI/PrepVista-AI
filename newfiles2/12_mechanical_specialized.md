### CAD / CAE Engineer — mechanical · mech · core/product · fresher→
Creates 3D models and runs engineering simulations.
**STACK** — Core (5): **CAD modeling** (SolidWorks/CATIA/Creo/NX — part/assembly/drawing, features, GD&T) · **FEA** (meshing, boundary conditions, static/modal/thermal, convergence) · strength of materials · **CFD basics** (flow simulation) · engineering drawing. Tools (3): SolidWorks/ANSYS/Abaqus · Hypermesh · GD&T. Emerging (2): topology optimization · generative design · digital twin.
**ROUNDS** — technical → CAD/CAE depth → project+HR.
**ASKABLE** — *CAD:* `[E]` sketch → feature workflow; extrude/revolve. `[M]` assembly mates/constraints; top-down vs bottom-up; GD&T; design intent. `[H]` complex surfacing; parametric/configurations. *FEA:* `[E]` what is FEA/meshing. `[M]` boundary conditions; element types; mesh quality; static vs modal vs thermal analysis; interpret stress/deformation results. `[H]` mesh convergence; nonlinear/contact; validate a simulation. *CFD:* `[M]` laminar vs turbulent setup; boundary conditions. `[H]` mesh independence; turbulence models.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: modeling + simulation setup + result validation. Weak: button-pushing, no BC/validation sense. **RED FLAGS** — trusts FEA without convergence/validation.

### Thermal Engineer — mechanical · mech · core/product · fresher→
Designs thermal-management and heat-transfer systems.
**STACK** — Core (5): **heat transfer** (conduction, convection, radiation, fins, HX) · **thermodynamics** (cycles, entropy, exergy) · **fluid mechanics** · thermal simulation (CFD/thermal FEA) · thermal-management design (cooling, heat sinks). Tools (3): ANSYS Fluent/Icepak · MATLAB · CAD. Emerging (2): electronics cooling · battery thermal · phase-change.
**ROUNDS** — technical → thermal/HT depth → project+HR.
**ASKABLE** — *Heat transfer:* `[E]` three modes; Fourier's law. `[M]` conduction through a wall/composite; convection (h, Nusselt); fins (efficiency); heat exchanger (LMTD, effectiveness-NTU). `[H]` transient conduction (Biot/Fourier number); radiation exchange; thermal resistance networks. *Thermo:* `[M]` cycles (Rankine/Brayton/refrigeration); COP; entropy. `[H]` exergy; real-cycle losses. *Design:* `[M]` size a heat sink; cooling for electronics; select a HX. `[H]` thermal simulation setup + validation.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: heat-transfer fundamentals + design + simulation. Weak: rote, no design application. **RED FLAGS** — confuses the three modes; no thermal-resistance thinking.

### HVAC Engineer — mechanical · mech · core/general · fresher→
Designs heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning systems.
**STACK** — Core (5): **refrigeration cycle** (vapor compression, components, refrigerants) · **psychrometrics** (humidity, dew point, psychrometric chart) · **load calculation** (heat load, cooling/heating) · **air distribution** (ducts, diffusers, airflow) · thermodynamics + heat transfer + fluid mechanics. Tools (3): HAP/Carrier · Revit MEP · AutoCAD. Emerging (2): energy efficiency/green HVAC · VRF · BMS.
**ROUNDS** — technical → HVAC depth → project+HR.
**ASKABLE** — *Refrigeration:* `[E]` vapor-compression components; what is a refrigerant. `[M]` refrigeration cycle on P-h; COP; refrigerant selection; superheat/subcooling. `[H]` cascade/multistage; efficiency improvement. *Psychrometrics:* `[E]` dry-bulb vs wet-bulb; humidity. `[M]` read a psychrometric chart; sensible vs latent heat; dew point; processes (cooling/heating/humidification). `[H]` bypass factor; ADP. *Load/ducts:* `[M]` cooling load components; duct sizing; airflow (CFM); tons of refrigeration. `[H]` load calc for a space; VAV vs CAV; static pressure.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: cycle + psychrometrics + load design. Weak: rote, can't read a psych chart. **RED FLAGS** — no psychrometrics; no load-calc sense.

**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: engine + dynamics + powertrain integration. Weak: rote, no system view. **RED FLAGS** — no cycle/performance understanding.

**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: aero + propulsion + structures fundamentals. Weak: rote formulas. **RED FLAGS** — no lift/drag/stability understanding.
