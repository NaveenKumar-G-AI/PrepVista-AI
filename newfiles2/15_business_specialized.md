### Technical Program Manager (TPM) — business_product · cse/aids/ece · product/general · fresher→
Drives cross-team technical programs end to end.
**STACK** — Core (5): **program/project management** (planning, dependencies, risk, timelines, RACI) · **technical fluency** (systems, APIs, architecture — enough to challenge engineers) · **execution/prioritization** (trade-offs, scope, critical path) · **stakeholder management/communication** (align teams, drive decisions) · metrics/data basics. Tools (3): Jira · roadmapping · dashboards. Emerging (2): AI program delivery · platform/infra programs.
**ROUNDS** — program/execution case → technical understanding → behavioral/cross-functional.
**ASKABLE** — *Program mgmt:* `[M]` how would you plan/drive <a cross-team launch>; identify dependencies + risks; handle a slipping timeline; what's on the critical path; RACI. `[H]` a program with many teams + conflicting priorities; recover a delayed program. *Technical:* `[M]` explain a system/architecture; ask the right questions of an eng team; assess technical risk. `[H]` trade-off a technical decision's impact on timeline. *Behavioral:* drive alignment without authority; a conflict between teams; a program that went off track + what you did; communicating to execs.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: structured execution + technical credibility + driving alignment. Weak: pure PM with no technical depth, or vague on execution. **RED FLAGS** — no dependency/risk thinking; can't engage technically.

### Program Manager / Project Manager (non-technical) — business_product · any · service/general · fresher→
Plans and delivers projects/programs.
**STACK** — Core (5): project management (scope, schedule, budget, risk, quality), methodologies (Agile/Scrum, Waterfall), stakeholder management, communication, tools (Jira/MS Project). `[M]` plan a project; CPM/critical path; risk register; manage scope creep; Agile vs Waterfall; sprint/backlog. `[H]` recover a delayed project; earned value; conflicting stakeholders; change management.
**STRONG/WEAK / RED FLAGS** — Strong: structured planning + stakeholder skill. Red flag: no risk/scope/critical-path structure.

### Strategy Analyst — business_product · mba/commerce/any · analytics/general · fresher→
Supports business strategy with analysis.
**STACK** — Core (5): **strategic frameworks** (Porter's 5 forces, SWOT, BCG matrix, value chain) · **business/financial analysis** (unit economics, market sizing) · **case problem-solving** (structured, hypothesis-led) · data/Excel · communication/storytelling. Tools (3): Excel · PowerPoint · analytics. Emerging (2): data-driven strategy · AI/digital strategy.
**ROUNDS** — strategy case → analytical → behavioral.
**ASKABLE** — *Frameworks/case:* `[M]` "should the company enter <market>"; competitive analysis (5 forces); "revenue is flat — where do we grow"; build vs buy; prioritize initiatives. `[H]` respond to a disruptor; portfolio/resource allocation; a strategy under uncertainty. *Analytical:* `[M]` market sizing; unit economics; interpret data for a decision. `[H]` financial impact of a strategy. *Communication:* structure a recommendation top-down.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: structured, framework-fluent-but-not-robotic, data-backed recommendation. Weak: framework-dumping, no data/recommendation. **RED FLAGS** — no structure; can't quantify; no clear recommendation.

### Growth Analyst / Marketing Analyst — business_product/data_ai · aids/mba/any · product/analytics/general · fresher→
Data-driven growth and marketing.
**STACK** — Core (5): **SQL + analytics** · **growth/marketing metrics** (CAC, LTV, ROAS, conversion, funnel, cohort, churn) · **experimentation (A/B, holdouts)** · **channels** (SEO/SEM, paid, email, lifecycle) · data viz. Tools (3): SQL · GA/Amplitude · Excel/Looker. Emerging (2): marketing automation · attribution modeling · AI in marketing.
**ROUNDS** — SQL/analytics → growth/marketing case → A/B → behavioral.
**ASKABLE** — *Metrics:* `[E]` what is CAC/LTV/conversion. `[M]` LTV:CAC; ROAS; funnel analysis; cohort/retention; channel performance; attribution. `[H]` optimize growth given a budget; diagnose a drop in conversions/signups. *Experimentation:* `[M]` design a growth experiment; measure a campaign's incremental impact; holdout groups. `[H]` attribution challenges; novelty effects. *Channels:* `[M]` SEO vs SEM vs paid vs organic; when each; email/lifecycle. `[H]` channel mix optimization. *SQL:* funnels, cohorts, retention.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: SQL + growth intuition + experimentation rigor. Weak: vanity metrics, no experimentation. **RED FLAGS** — no CAC/LTV understanding; no incrementality thinking.

### Business Development Associate / Category / Operations Manager — business_product · mba/any · general/service · fresher→
Business development / category & operations management.
**STACK & QUESTIONS** —
- **BD Associate:** core: sales fundamentals, market research, negotiation, relationship building, business acumen, communication. `[E]` how would you pitch <product>. `[M]` identify/qualify leads; negotiation basics; market entry for a product; handle an objection. `[H]` build a BD strategy; partnership structuring.
- **Category/Operations Manager:** core: category management (assortment, pricing, margins), operations/supply, analytics, vendor management, P&L. `[E]` what is a category/margin. `[M]` assortment/pricing decisions; inventory/demand; vendor negotiation; improve category P&L. `[H]` category strategy; ops optimization; trade-offs (margin vs volume).
**STRONG/WEAK / RED FLAGS** — Strong: commercial acumen + structure + numbers. Red flag: no P&L/negotiation/structure sense.

### Supply-Chain Analyst / Customer Success Manager / Process Consultant — business_product · any · general/service · fresher→
Supply chain analytics / customer success / process consulting.
**STACK & QUESTIONS** —
- **Supply-Chain Analyst:** core: inventory (EOQ, safety stock), forecasting, logistics, SQL/Excel, S&OP, analytics. `[M]` EOQ/reorder point; demand forecasting; bullwhip effect; reduce inventory cost; SQL/Excel analysis. `[H]` network optimization; supply-demand balancing.
- **Customer Success Manager:** core: customer lifecycle, onboarding, retention/churn, relationship management, product knowledge, metrics (NRR, churn, health score). `[E]` what is churn/retention. `[M]` onboard a customer; identify churn risk (health score); handle an unhappy customer; drive adoption/upsell. `[H]` reduce churn for a segment; QBR strategy.
- **Process Consultant:** core: process mapping, Lean/Six Sigma, gap analysis, RCA, change management. `[M]` map/improve a process; RCA; measure improvement; stakeholder buy-in. `[H]` transformation with trade-offs.
**STRONG/WEAK / RED FLAGS** — Strong: structured + metrics + people/process sense. Red flag: no method/metrics; vague.
