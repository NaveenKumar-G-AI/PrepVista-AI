### Geotechnical Engineer — civil · civil · core/general · fresher→
Analyzes soil and designs foundations/earthworks.
**STACK** — Core (5): **soil mechanics** (classification, phase relations, permeability, consolidation, shear strength c-φ) · **foundation engineering** (shallow/deep, bearing capacity, settlement, pile design) · **earth pressure** (active/passive, retaining walls) · **slope stability** · site investigation (SPT, boring, lab tests). Tools (3): GeoStudio/PLAXIS · lab tests · AutoCAD. Emerging (2): ground improvement · geosynthetics.
**ROUNDS** — technical → geotech depth → project+HR.
**ASKABLE** — *Soil mechanics:* `[E]` soil types; phase diagram (void ratio, porosity). `[M]` soil classification (USCS/IS); permeability (Darcy); consolidation (primary/secondary); shear strength (Mohr-Coulomb, c & φ); effective stress. `[H]` consolidation settlement calc; triaxial test interpretation. *Foundations:* `[E]` shallow vs deep. `[M]` bearing capacity (Terzaghi); settlement; when piles; pile capacity (skin + end). `[H]` foundation on weak/expansive soil; group pile efficiency. *Earth pressure/slope:* `[M]` active vs passive; retaining-wall stability (overturning/sliding); FoS for slopes. `[H]` slope stability methods.
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: effective stress + bearing/settlement + FoS. Weak: rote, no effective-stress concept. **RED FLAGS** — confuses total/effective stress; no FoS sense.

### Transportation / Highway Engineer — civil · civil · core/general · fresher→
Designs roads, highways, and transportation systems.
**STACK** — Core (5): **highway geometric design** (alignment, curves, superelevation, sight distance) · **pavement design** (flexible/rigid, layers, IRC methods) · **traffic engineering** (volume, capacity, LOS, signals) · **materials** (bitumen, aggregates, tests) · surveying. Tools (3): MX Road/Civil 3D · AutoCAD. Emerging (2): ITS · sustainable pavements.
**ROUNDS** — technical → transportation depth → project+HR.
**ASKABLE** — *Geometric design:* `[E]` types of curves; what is superelevation. `[M]` horizontal/vertical curves; sight distance (SSD/OSD); superelevation design; gradient. `[H]` transition curves; design speed relationships. *Pavement:* `[E]` flexible vs rigid pavement. `[M]` pavement layers/functions; CBR method; IRC design; failures. `[H]` rigid pavement stresses; overlay design. *Traffic:* `[M]` traffic volume/PCU; capacity & LOS; signal design (cycle time). `[H]` intersection design; capacity analysis. *Materials:* `[M]` bitumen tests (penetration/ductility); aggregate tests; mix design (Marshall).
**STRONG/WEAK** — Strong: geometric + pavement + traffic. Weak: rote formulas. **RED FLAGS** — no sight-distance/pavement understanding.

### Environmental Engineer / Water Resources Engineer — civil · civil · core/general · fresher→
Designs water/wastewater and hydraulic systems.
**STACK & QUESTIONS** —
- **Environmental:** core: water treatment (coagulation, filtration, disinfection), wastewater treatment (primary/secondary/tertiary, BOD/COD, activated sludge), air/solid-waste, standards. `[E]` water vs wastewater treatment. `[M]` treatment stages; BOD vs COD; activated sludge; disinfection (chlorination); DO sag. `[H]` design a treatment unit; sludge treatment; effluent standards.
- **Water Resources:** core: hydrology (rainfall-runoff, hydrographs), hydraulics (open channel, pipe flow), irrigation, dams, groundwater. `[E]` hydrologic cycle. `[M]` rainfall-runoff; unit hydrograph; open-channel flow (Manning); reservoir capacity; irrigation water requirement. `[H]` flood routing; groundwater flow; dam design basics.
**STRONG/WEAK / RED FLAGS** — Strong: process/hydraulic fundamentals + design. Weak: rote. Red flag: no BOD/hydrograph/open-channel understanding.

### Surveying Engineer / Estimation & Quantity Surveyor — civil · civil · core/general · fresher→
Surveying / cost estimation & quantities.
**STACK & QUESTIONS** —
- **Surveying:** core: levelling, theodolite/total station, traversing, contouring, GPS, curve setting, errors/corrections. `[E]` levelling; chain vs total station. `[M]` traversing + closing error; contouring; corrections; curve setting out. `[H]` triangulation; GPS/GNSS workflows.
- **Estimation/QS:** core: quantity takeoff, rate analysis, BOQ, specifications, measurement (IS 1200), costing, tendering. `[E]` what is a BOQ; rate analysis. `[M]` estimate quantities (concrete/steel/brickwork) for an element; rate analysis; measurement rules; contingencies. `[H]` detailed estimate for a structure; cost optimization; valuation.
**STRONG/WEAK / RED FLAGS** — Strong: accuracy + practical measurement/costing. Weak: no measurement/correction method. Red flag: careless quantities; no error correction.
